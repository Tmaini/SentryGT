    The problem

    Scams don't announce themselves.

    Most people don't fall for scams because they're careless — they fall for them because a malicious link is designed to look exactly like the real thing, arriving at exactly the wrong moment. By the time something feels off, the click already happened. Sentry moves that moment of doubt earlier, so it happens before the click instead of after.

    How it works

    Three checks, before you ever tap.

    1. 01

      A link or file reaches you

      Text, email, DM, download — Sentry watches the moment something new arrives, without reading your actual conversations.

    2. 02

      Sentry checks it instantly

      Domain age, known scam patterns, and mismatched branding are checked in real time — in the background, before you interact with it.

    3. 03

      You get a plain-language warning

      No jargon, no security-team speak — just a clear heads-up on what looks wrong and why, so you can decide with the full picture.
